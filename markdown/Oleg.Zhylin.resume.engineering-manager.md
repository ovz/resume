## My Story

I began in **cryptography and security engineering**, working for my university mentors while completing my Data Security degree. That training still shapes how I read a system: what can go wrong, what evidence we have, and whether the proposed control addresses the problem.

At Salford Systems I grew from product engineer into architect, product owner and leader of international development teams. I learned to give people a clear technical direction and enough ownership to improve it. Through the acquisition by Minitab, I carried the product knowledge, engineering records and onboarding process into a larger organization.

I now own architecture decisions across wearable and handset product lines. I want to carry that technical depth into engineering management, helping people deliver, make sound trade-offs and leave the systems they own easier to maintain.
