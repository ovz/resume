* ***Embedded software sold off the shelf***. The devices I build software for are retail products. [Lively Mobile+][r4] was [sold in Best Buy and Walmart stores][cpsc_recall] from its 2019 launch, and [Lively Mobile 2][r5] is [sold in stores][lively_store_locator] as well. Best Buy gave Lively [two aisle endcaps in 2020, and by 2021 most of a store's main prepaid display space][lively_bby_display]. The service behind the devices had [more than 900,000 paying subscribers][bby_greatcall] when Best Buy acquired GreatCall in 2018. I could walk into a store, watch customers try the device, and talk about my work with the associates and the store general manager. Few embedded engineers see their software on a shelf, or meet the people who buy it. For me it was a regular reminder of who depends on it.
