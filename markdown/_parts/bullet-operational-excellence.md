* ***Operational Excellence** and **Observability** at fleet scale*. I co-designed an **agentless** device-health architecture for a cellular-connected embedded fleet after establishing that no conventional monitoring agent could fit the device's memory budget, then built the **Monitoring** layer on top of it: production **Dashboards**, tuned **Alerts**, and **Anomaly Detection** derived from **ARIMA/SARIMA** first principles and partitioned by error category to suppress false positives. This caught device and firmware defects before customers reported them, and turned broad fleet signals into focused, device-level engineering cases. I own that monitor portfolio as a *product* — retiring superseded coverage, validating alerts against independent firmware telemetry, and presenting the practice to a cross-team engineering community.
