# On SEPARATION AND GENERAL RELEASE AGREEMENT

## From Oleg Zhylin ("*Zhylin*")

## To Minitab, Inc. ("*Minitab*")

----

Thank you very much for offering a SEPARATION AND GENERAL RELEASE AGREEMENT. I share the goal to settle all the claims to mutual satisfaction.

The agreement gave me a chance to make a thorough assessment of Salford Systems employment history as well as career paths in front of me. As admonished in the proposed agreement, I have consulted at length with legal counsel. I take very seriously my obligation to provide for and support those who most depend on me, my wife and children. Accordingly, I and my wife together have deliberated on this very important chapter in our lives presented by my separation from Minitab. I am prepared to sign the Separation Agreement with the following changes:

* Lump sum severance payment for the countered sum of **$172,800** (one hundred and seventy-two thousands eight hundred).
* The recommendation letter from Minitab as related in the agreement paragraph 5(C). Please see a draft in attachment to this letter.

I regret that Minitab and I are going separate ways. However, life being a journey, I hope matters can be resolved amicably and Minitab and I may each move forward and prosper.

Please review and advise for the consummation of this chapter in our respective lives.

**Below I elaborate on some observations on past and present circumstances. I sincerely hope this will help our mutual decision to leave all the controversies behind.**

----

>&nbsp;
>

My family is eternally grateful for having the fortune to move to the United States from Ukraine. Salford Systems was instrumental in this life-changing experience. None of us felt more at home in our lives than here and now. My wife and I are so glad that we can keep our children away from horrible experiences everyone goes through during war in Ukraine.

Given my almost 20 years of work for Salford Systems and then Minitab, I am very fearful I will be unemployed for quite some time. Severance payment will ensure that I can continue to provide for my children Yelyzaveta (daughter, 10 years old) and Bogdan (son, 5 years old) so they grow up into healthy and productive members of American society. My family is also fortunate to have all four grandparents in good health and active. They all live in Ukraine. Eventually, they will need our help and care. This unconditionally is part of our life plan. I was sure that I would be able to provide for them while working; now I am not so sure.

I was affiliated with Salford Systems in various capacities for almost two decades. I contributed my engineering talent all over the software codebase. I actively participated in the evolution of software products. Many of my ideas created the commercial success that Salford Systems products enjoy.

During all these years I had a chance to communicate with other professionals outside the company during trainings, road shows, and other professional networking events. I used every chance to promote the Business. It was easy given our unique value proposition and an interesting challenge to carry over deep, non-trivial, scientific nature of our products.

In many ways, I was instrumental in creating the *Intellectual Property* that motivated Minitab to acquire Salford Systems.

With the end of the Salford Systems era of my life, I have a lot of work in front of me. The bulk of my experience is working long term for a Business and bringing it to incredible heights. This what I know best how to do. I am almost 40 years old and I most likely will have only a single chance to build up a career comparable to the one I had with Salford Systems. But given the single company
I wholeheartedly welcomed the relationship when Salford Systems became a Minitab company. Joining the Minitab family was a big step forward. I am sure the future of Salford Systems is bright. I eagerly and enthusiastically collaborated with Minitab teams to make sure every piece of value that Minitab acquired received proper governance. I used my best professional and personal judgment to develop the business, apply the best technological solutions and make co-workers with both Minitab and Salford Systems backgrounds a single and effective team.

During past months I was prevented from the fulfillment of my professional duties. For reasons that were kept secret from me the development of *Salford Predictive Modeler (SPM)* software product stagnated. Instead of using the momentum we got after the acquisition and delivering one successful product release after another, the team was stuck with non-essential tasks. I brought this up regularly during One-on-One meetings with my direct supervisor. My offers to contribute and collaborate were often ignored and silently rejected.

*I always respected the decision-making process established by the organization. I fully agree that making decisions equals taking Responsibility and Risk. Back during small company days, all the decisions were made by the Business Owner. I contributed my best abilities to provide solutions. Going back decades I have a track record of the top level of professionalism executing projects that moved Salford Systems forward.*

I understand fully and entirely that knowledge and experience of Salford Systems engineers are a key part of what Minitab acquired with the merger. I took the team integration process very responsibly and used my best professional judgment. However, the *Engineering Department* members were noticeably unwilling to work as a single team. They kept their distance, differentiating between people with Minitab and Salford Systems background. Engineering practices common in the industry to prototype rapidly and represent ideas as Pull Requests and Demo Projects met unjustified pushbacks.

Despite what was stated in ***"Paid Administrative Leave and Termination of Employment"** letter hand delivered to me on May 29, 2018,* dialog was severely lacking. I never received an adequate performance evaluation or feedback.

It was clear to me that the acquisition of Salford Systems was a new experience for Minitab. I was looking forward very much to get past the transition and collaborate effectively to reach our business goals. For reasons unknown to me, Minitab decided instead to terminate my employment.

I strongly suspect the reasons for unprofessional behavior by co-workers were personal. There is a history going back years of derogatory comments and condescending attitude about my appearance, eating habits, English language skills etc. This behavior did not disappear after the merger, and during the last months became much worse.

* I was rudely interrupted when I was trying to participate in a professional conversation.
* Other co-workers received preferential treatment. This was deliberately done during team meetings.
* I received aggressive and irritated comments about my work mode, eating habits etc.
* Co-workers deliberately pretended not to understand my accent. English pronunciation is notoriously challenging for speakers of the Russian language. I am always aware of this and make a conscious effort to get myself understood. Despite my experience of successful communication with Salford Systems team members over decades, lately I experienced a good deal of negativity in response to expressing my thoughts.
* Despite Minitab not having a dress code I received mocking and disrespectful comments about my attire.
* Maintaining a healthy body is one of the core principles of my lifestyle. I was always conscious to make sure that keeping my body in tone did not interfere with anyone. Nevertheless, I received unpleasant and sometimes aggressive comments.

The behaviors I encountered were easily recognizable as discriminatory. Anything “out of the norm” about my person was used to attempt inflicting psychological pain to me.

I approached my job in a professional and compassionate manner. I explicitly re-assured co-workers that I treat all human beings with the utmost respect regardless of their roles and achievements. In a work environment, professionalism is expected to be a dominating principle.

In the culture I grew up in, it is *vital* to persevere through challenges. There is no trusted authority to fall back upon.  The first thing on my mind when I encounter a problem is to come up with action items to get it resolved. I made significant efforts to bring things firmly on a professional basis. Regretfully this did not happen.

I did mention a lack of progress in Product Development and other goals in One on One meetings with my direct supervisor. I shared my vision and thoughts and articulated how I planned to deal with the situations in the most professional and compassionate manner. I received no feedback, support, or advise.

For the reasons stated above, I experienced a great psychological burden after the termination. I have to overcome it to build my future professional career. It is a challenge to let these issues go and focus on preparing for my next employment. Some of the challenges do require time to heal and this impairs my ability to embark successfully on a new career.

*I wish the best of luck to Minitab and the team in building a successful and prosperous business. Please feel free to reach out if I can help with anything.*

**Thank You!**
