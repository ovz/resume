# ○ Agentic engineering platform

> **Doc type:** reference · **origin: suggested** — proposed by an agent on 2026-09-13 from this repository's 2023-2026 AI record plus outside research. Not the owner's idea.

## The job

Building the platform that makes AI agents a dependable capability for an engineering organization rather than a personal trick: orchestration and delegation, context and knowledge systems, token economics, evaluation, guardrails, and the integration points where agents actually land work — code review, documentation, release notes, CI. The users are engineers inside the company, which makes it platform engineering with a reliability budget.

## Under the Value and Impact tests

**Enabler, not product — unless the employer sells the tooling.** Inside a product company this platform makes engineers faster: a real differential, but one visible mainly to engineers and funded as overhead, which is where the [Impact test](dream-job-hub.md) stops it. At a company whose product *is* agent infrastructure, identical work sits on the revenue line. **That distinction should decide which postings are worth answering**, and it is more predictive here than seniority or scope.

## Why it is a real field

The titles are barely a year old and already in postings — *Agent Engineer* (described as formalised in late 2025 alongside the Claude Agent SDK, Google's ADK and LangGraph), *Agent Systems Engineer*, *Agentic AI Engineer* — and at the senior end an AI-platform leadership role whose most-cited responsibility is "multi-agent orchestration, model routing, and runtime controls for non-deterministic systems". Context engineering is repeatedly named as where the hard problems are, and as early enough that depth compounds. Sources: [specializations landscape](../analysis/2026-09-13-specializations-landscape.md) § *Agentic AI and AI platform engineering*.

**The caution belongs on the page too:** titles this new are unstable, and the substance of a posting ranges from prompt plumbing to real platform work. The screen: does the work have users inside the engineering organization, and a reliability budget?

## What the record already supports

He built this before the titles existed:

- **A multi-agent orchestration layer shipped as an agent plugin**, preventing context overflow through delegation, plus **a persistent, versioned knowledge base for the engineering organization** — [2026-05-26](../../raw/brag/2026-05-26-ai-adoption-agentic-engineering-choreographer.md). The same entry records prompt and agent patterns that cut token consumption while holding output quality, and agents integrated into code review, documentation and release-note workflows.
- **Agents treated as contributors with a route to land work**: [CI configured so an AI coding agent could open pull requests against the test-automation repository](../../raw/brag/2024-12-31-device-test-automation-robot-framework.md) — the difference between a suggestion box and a contributor.
- **Token economics as an engineering concern**, including deliberate model selection for a task and measured budgets — his own 2026 working notes treat "does this task justify this model" as a routine question.
- **AI-assisted rapid prototyping to become productive on an unfamiliar platform**, and to judge an idea's innovation potential early — both stated as current architecture-ownership practice ([resume][primary]).
- **Knowledge-system design as the differentiator**: raw capture → synthesis → index, versioned, with agent instructions that survive context loss. This repository is itself an instance, which is a demonstrable artifact rather than a claim.
- **Seventeen years of ML product work**, which means he evaluates model behaviour as a product problem and is not impressed by demos.

## The gap, and the shortest path

**The gap is scale and externality.** The work is real but its blast radius is one engineering organization, and much of the evidence is internal. Nothing here has been evaluated by outsiders, published, or run at the scale a platform team would ask about.

Shortest path: publish one artifact — an open-sourced orchestration pattern, a written account of the token-budget results, or the knowledge-system pattern as a reusable skill — and attach numbers to the claims that currently rest on assertion. The [retired productivity multiplier](../resume/coverage.md) is the cautionary example: a quantified claim with no measurement behind it had to be withdrawn, and this candidate's credibility depends on not repeating that.

## How to tell if this is the one

**Check where the satisfaction sits.** If it is in other engineers becoming faster — the platform outcome — this is the one. If it is in the systems he builds *with* the agents (devices, engines, fusion), then AI is his tooling rather than his field, and this candidate is a way of working rather than a job. The 2026 record reads, honestly, like both.
