## Battery, power and cost of operation

- Carries **battery and power management as a second specialization alongside positioning**, and established that on a cellular wearable they are one subject: the fallback breadcrumbing interval is specified in units of MQTT keep-alive intervals for power-efficiency reasons, so a location report rides an existing network wake-up instead of paying for one. [2021-10-16-battery-power-second-specialization](../../raw/brag/2021-10-16-battery-power-second-specialization.md)
- Holds a platform-level intuition for what consumes power on Qualcomm MDM-class SoCs — which positioning methods the modem serves alone, what waking the application processor costs against running an MCU continuously, combo-radio beacon scanning, wakelocks, cellular power-saving modes — and the transferable half of it: capable hardware costs power twice, the wake-up chain starts as low in the stack as possible, and a power claim without a measurement is an opinion. [2021-10-16-battery-power-second-specialization](../../raw/brag/2021-10-16-battery-power-second-specialization.md)
- Advises product management and business partners on what will and will not move battery life before a quarter is spent finding out, using a stated hypothesis and a cheap experiment — an engineering build, a few devices soaking, a notebook — rather than an analysis campaign; delivered the 2026 keep-alive production configuration that way. [2021-10-16-battery-power-second-specialization](../../raw/brag/2021-10-16-battery-power-second-specialization.md)
- Found devices burning real money in cellular data on the company's own MVNO by joining carrier cellular-operations data, device telemetry and warehouse event tables on device identity; validated the detection path by **building a rogue device** — an emulation script driving deliberate excessive consumption — so the threshold was agreed before an incident rather than during one. [2024-01-04-cellular-cost-rogue-device-detection](../../raw/brag/2024-01-04-cellular-cost-rogue-device-detection.md)
- Published a rogue-device runbook and fed the scenario into the launch tabletop exercise; the work then revealed a real unit at roughly a gigabyte a day, and replacement *plus* root cause became the routine response so each bad device taught the fleet something. [2024-01-04-cellular-cost-rogue-device-detection](../../raw/brag/2024-01-04-cellular-cost-rogue-device-detection.md)
- Holds the power budget to the industry's standard of care — duty-cycle-weighted average current built from the right datasheet rows, component behaviour *observed* on discovery hardware rather than taken from a feature list, estimates confirmed by measurement (owner's correction of emphasis, 2026-09-24). [2021-11-15-r5-product-architecture-power-budget-tradeoffs](../../raw/brag/2021-11-15-r5-product-architecture-power-budget-tradeoffs.md)
