# I could walk into a Best Buy and see the device I write software for

## Why I still care

In the owner's words: seeing the device in the store "added powerful reinforcement of my meaningful work". Embedded software almost never meets the person it serves. This did, two generations running, and it is the plainest answer he has to "why this work".

**The cue:** the Lively display at the front of the store, on the line with Apple, Amazon and Google. *(Owner to add one visit: which store, roughly when, and one thing a customer or an associate said. That is the image to rehearse from; until it is supplied, beats 4 and 6 are agent-drafted.)*

## Register

The GreatCall and Best Buy Health eras: plain, economical, no drama. The shelf space was Best Buy merchandising's decision, not his, so the story never claims it. What is his is the work in the box and the choice to go and look. See [voice and prominence](../../workflows/voice-and-prominence.md).

## Narrative — rehearse verbatim

*Agent-drafted from the owner's two statements of 2026-10-01; no dictation of a single visit is on record yet. Replace beat 4's middle line with his own memory once he has written it down.*

**0 · Offer**
> There is one part of this job that I don't think most embedded engineers get to have.

**1 · Hook**
> The device I write software for is sold at Best Buy, and I can walk into a store and see it.
⟨breathe⟩

**2 · Stakes**
> It is an emergency-response device for seniors, with a help button and fall detection. Usually it is a son or a daughter buying it for a parent.
*(optional)* When Best Buy bought GreatCall in 2018, the service had more than nine hundred thousand paying subscribers.

**3 · Complication**
> In my experience embedded work is mostly invisible. On a normal day my customers are a contract manufacturer, a specification, and a dashboard of fleet telemetry.
*(optional)* Our first retail launch was in April 2019, and the recall came that August. So I never took the shelf for granted.
⟨breathe⟩

**4 · Move**
> So I went to the store. The Lively display was on the front line, next to Apple, Amazon and Google. I watched people pick the device up and try it, and I talked with the store associates about what it does. Once I talked with the store general manager about the work I do.
*(optional)* In 2020 Best Buy gave Lively two endcaps, one for phones and one for wearables. A year later an industry analyst found Lively holding most of the main prepaid display space in a store.

**5 · Punchline**
> Two generations of that device have been sold off the shelf since 2019. For me it was a regular reminder of who depends on the work.
⟨breathe⟩

**6 · Handover**
> Have you ever had a chance to watch someone buy something you built?

## If they follow up

- **"How many devices are out there?"** → The public number is the service, not the device: more than 900,000 paying subscribers at the 2018 acquisition. The 2019 recall notice covered about 44,000 units of the first build. I don't quote internal counts.
- **"What happened with the recall?"** → That is its own story — the August 2019 CPSC recall and relaunch. Offer it.
- **"Did anything from the store change the product?"** → *Owner to answer from memory.* If nothing did, say so: the visits were about seeing the customer, not collecting requirements.
- **"Is it still on sale?"** → Lively Mobile 2 is, at Best Buy and through Lively's store locator. The first generation's Best Buy listing appears to have ended late in 2025.
- **"Why does it matter to you?"** → Hand over to [Why I do this work](why-i-do-this-work.md): he does his best work when he can see who depends on it.

## Proof

All in the source entry's *The public record*, each a pinned Wayback capture:

- CPSC recall 19-775: Lively Mobile Plus sold in Best Buy and Walmart stores, April–May 2019.
- Wave7 Research via BestMVNO, 16 August 2021: two Lively endcaps from Q1 2020; most of a Kansas store's main prepaid display space by August 2021.
- Lively's store locator, December 2025: Lively Mobile 2 can be browsed, bought and activated in person.
- Best Buy, 15 August 2018: GreatCall acquired with more than 900,000 paying subscribers.
- BestBuy.com product listings for both generations, 2020–2026.

## Know it — what stays with me

T1, never said: the internal model number on Lively Mobile 2's Best Buy listing is R5-RTL-BLK-8IN, so the generation name is public but there is no reason to say it. The owner recalls about a million confirmed active lines of service in the enterprise data warehouse; that figure is internal and stays out of the room — use the public 900,000. "Lively Fab" is his name for the display; its meaning is not yet recorded. The neighbouring brands are his memory, not a public fact, which is fine for a first-person story and not fine for a written claim.
