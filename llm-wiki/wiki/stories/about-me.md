# About me — why I do this work, and ownership — story cluster

> **Doc type:** reference
>
> Hub for the About Me stories: the "why" story and the bank of short ownership stories. Source: the owner's words of 2026-09-23 (theme of belonging, meaningful work, stewardship and ownership), received as a mailbox message during interview preparation. **Staged for the owner's review** — none of this is final voice. Audience: the owner preparing to be asked "why this work, why now"; agents writing outward text, who must apply [voice and prominence](../workflows/voice-and-prominence.md) first.

## The through-line

He does his best work when he can see who depends on it, and he wants his stewardship to reach further: own the risk on the critical path, among strong peers, judged on results. The theme complements the [stewardship cluster](stewardship.md) (the practice) and the [dream-job hub](../dream-jobs/dream-job-hub.md#mission-belonging-and-ownership--the-register-underneath-the-grades) (the target).
