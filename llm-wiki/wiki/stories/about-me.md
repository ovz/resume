# About me — why I do this work, and ownership — story cluster

> **Doc type:** reference
>
> Hub for the About Me stories: the "why" story, the bank of short ownership stories, and the map from the ownership and autonomy behaviours to the stories that show them. Sources: the owner's words of 2026-09-23 (theme of belonging, meaningful work, stewardship and ownership), received as a mailbox message during interview preparation, and of 2026-10-02 (autonomy, results made known, credit handed to the people who receive them). **Staged for the owner's review** — none of this is final voice. Audience: the owner preparing to be asked "why this work, why now"; agents writing outward text, who must apply [voice and prominence](../workflows/voice-and-prominence.md) first.

## The through-line

He does his best work when he can see who depends on it, and he wants his stewardship to reach further: own the risk on the critical path, among strong peers, judged on results. He works with autonomy and makes every result known, and he hands it on so the people who receive it can own it — that is how he integrates into a team, and it is what § *Ownership and autonomy* below maps to stories. The theme complements the [stewardship cluster](stewardship.md) (the practice) and the [dream-job hub](../dream-jobs/dream-job-hub.md#mission-belonging-and-ownership--the-register-underneath-the-grades) (the target).

### Telling it to a defence listener

Defence and government employers speak a vocabulary that already prizes these behaviours — the SOF Truths' *"Humans are more important than hardware"* and *"Quality is better than quantity"*, and mission command's *commander's intent*, *disciplined initiative* and *mutual trust* (sources checked and linked in the capability entry). The stories answer in those terms without being bent toward them:

| What the listener screens for | Lead with |
|---|---|
| Initiative inside someone else's intent | [AM8](about-me/the-assignment-i-spent-on-people.md), then [P4](positioning/home-away-kept-simple.md) |
| Reporting while acting; nobody surprised | [AM9](about-me/showing-the-monitors.md), then [AM3](about-me/the-mission-lesson.md) |
| Judgment and accepted risk | [C3](concurrency/the-compiler-was-wrong.md) or [C4](concurrency/the-gatekeeper.md), then [P1](positioning/buying-calendar-time.md) |
| Trust, and people before equipment | [SF5](salford/the-best-peer-collaboration.md), [AM2](about-me/percentiles-not-averages.md), [AM7](about-me/on-the-shelf.md) |
| Custody of something that must not fail | [ST1](stewardship/records-nobody-asked-for.md), [ST5](stewardship/a-control-someone-checks.md) |

Three rules hold for this listener. **Never imply military service** — none is on record. **Use their word only after they use it**; reciting doctrine to a defence interviewer is borrowed authority. **The lens stays private**: no story is framed against anything, and the owner's reading of throughput-driven organizations is for choosing employers, not for saying aloud.
