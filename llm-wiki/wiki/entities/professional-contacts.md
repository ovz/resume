# Professional Contacts

> **Doc type:** reference
>
> The owner's professional network as a working roster: who they are, how the working relationship ran, over what period, and whether they are available as a reference. This is the page to open before asking someone for a reference, before a networking round, or when a brag entry names a collaborator. Audience: the owner; agents updating [`markdown/Oleg.Zhylin.professional.references.md`](../../../markdown/Oleg.Zhylin.professional.references.md) or ingesting a brag entry that names someone.

## Scope

This page holds **name, role, organization, period, and the professional substance of the relationship**. It is a *roster* — the page you open to answer "who is this person to me, and can I ask them for a reference?" — and it is sized to be read in one pass.

**One hard exclusion: no contact details.** Phone numbers and email addresses live in one place only — the references document — and are never copied into a wiki page. Consent to be a reference is not consent to be indexed. See [sensitivity tiers](../workflows/sensitivity-tiers.md) rule 3.

**Everything else about a working relationship is recorded — just not necessarily here.** The narrative of how a collaboration actually went (what was disagreed about, how a mentoring relationship developed, how a team responded to something the owner introduced, what a performance or promotion conversation established) is a crucial part of the owner's professional experience and is captured in full at T1, per [sensitivity tiers](../workflows/sensitivity-tiers.md) rule 8. Its home is the brag entry or synthesis page covering that piece of work, where it sits in context. This page then links to it from the person's entry.

That division is about **page shape, not permission**. A roster that absorbed every relationship narrative would stop being a roster. If an ingest surfaces rich material about a working relationship, write it where the work is described and cross-link it here — do not drop it, and do not thin it out on the way.

Tier: **T1 private repo** throughout — this page names colleagues, which is fine at T1 and never at T0. Colleague names come out when anything is promoted to the public resume.

## Maintenance

- **One page for now.** When a contact accumulates enough genuine history to need its own page, split them into `entities/contacts/<name>.md` and leave a one-line pointer in the roster. Do not pre-create per-person pages for contacts with a single line of content — an empty note is worse than a table row.
- **Obsidian.** The vault opens on the repository root, so backlinks from brag entries to this page work as soon as an entry links a person here. When adding a contact who appears in a brag entry, add the link in both directions — that is what makes the graph view useful for spotting who your recent work actually involved.
- **Keep the roster and the references document in agreement.** They are two views of the same commitment; a person added to one and not the other is a defect.
- **Spelling and titles are load-bearing.** A misspelled name in a document handed to a recruiter is worse than an omitted one. Confirm with the owner before any name here reaches `markdown/`.
