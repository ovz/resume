## Best Buy Health (GreatCall), 2018–present — Sr. Software Engineer, Principal-level scope

GreatCall, an MVNO (now on Verizon) making cellular devices and services for seniors, became Best Buy Health after acquisition; the owner joined the embedded team for the Lively Mobile+ emergency-response device and has since worked across the Lively devices and apps line. Milestones stated in the primary: 2019 Lively Mobile+ relaunch with data-driven quality work; 2024 Lively Mobile 2 launch (UI, industrial and electrical design brought forward; Qualcomm Skyhook positioning on Qualcomm Linux Enablement); company positioning SME; automated fall detection; on-device test automation and QA enablement; Datadog-based operational excellence meeting an OKR; telemetry and process-monitoring designs; hiring-process contributions; AI-assisted engineering since 2023. [primary] Supervisor and peer references cover Oct 2019 – Apr 2022. [refs]

## Minitab, 2017–2018 — Sr. Advisory Software Engineer

Salford Systems became a Minitab company in 2017. The owner led the intellectual-property transfer and governance, the migration of code, issue tracking, and CI/CD to Visual Studio Team Services (completed in under a year), codebase quality and style alignment with other tech leads, onboarding acceleration (2–3 months → under a week), a stable SPM 8.3 baseline, test coverage, Nalpeiron licensing, and a NuGet reuse repository. [primary] The archived long-form adds the framing that both companies made heavy scientific technology usable for business users — Minitab for classical statistics, Salford for machine learning. ## Salford Systems, 2000–2017 — Sr. Software Engineer, Architect

A San Diego company that commercialized CART (Breiman, Friedman, Olshen, Stone) and later TreeNet (Friedman's gradient boosting) under founder Dan Steinberg. The owner joined as a contractor from Ukraine during his final university year, became the primary GUI developer for CART/SPM, then architect and product owner for the Qt GUI rewrite, Cloud-ready SPM, the Predictive engines API, and distributed ISLE; ran consulting and data-engineering engagements; managed distributed teams of up to 15 including outsourced developers in Ukraine and China.
