## Current synthesis

Oleg Zhylin is a software engineer, professionally active since 1996, whose work combines C++ and Python engineering, software architecture, technical leadership, data engineering, machine learning, embedded devices, and security. The through-line is turning technically difficult systems into dependable products that users and businesses can operate — and increasingly, doing so at Principal-level scope across a product line. Stated target: a Sr. Principal or Sr. Staff Engineer role with an employer interested in the work rather than the titles. [primary][primary]

Four chapters:

1. **Security and cryptography (1996–2000).** Undergraduate employment at IIT under university mentors: cryptographic libraries, full-disk encryption, elliptic-curve thesis. Left a durable security-professional lens. [primary][primary] [long][long]
2. **Machine learning products (2000–2017, Salford Systems).** Primary GUI developer and later architect/product owner for CART and Salford Predictive Modeler; consulting and data-engineering projects; distributed teams up to 15; cloud, big-data, API, and internationalization initiatives in the final years. [primary][primary] [long][long]
3. **Acquisition and integration (2017–2018, Minitab).** Intellectual-property transfer and governance, codebase and process quality, migration to Visual Studio Team Services in under a year. [primary][primary]
4. **Embedded emergency-response devices (2018–present, GreatCall → Best Buy Health).** Lively Mobile+ relaunch and Lively Mobile 2 launch; positioning SME; automated fall detection; on-device test automation; Datadog-based operational excellence meeting an OKR; AI-assisted engineering since 2023. [primary][primary]
