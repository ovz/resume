## Why it is not resume material

- The claims about the owner's contributions in the drafted letter are the owner's own words and are already stated, with better sourcing, in the resumes.
- The remaining content is personal (family), legal (separation terms), and grievance-related. None of it belongs in any outward-facing document, brag entry, or wiki synthesis, and it is precisely the kind of employer-relationship detail the [sensitivity tiers](../workflows/sensitivity-tiers.md) keep out of T0.
- The owner's Minitab tenure (2017–2018) and its professional content are fully covered by the [primary resume](resume-achievements.md) and the [long-form resume](resume-full.md).

## Recommendation (owner decision)

This directory is version-controlled today. Because it is personal/legal rather than career knowledge, and because the repo may one day be shared with a collaborator, the recommended disposition is to **remove it from version control** — move it to the maintainer scope's never-committed `raw/` area or to an offline backup — and keep only this page as the record that it exists. If the owner prefers to keep it committed, no wiki page should link into its contents beyond this one.
