## What it is

A condensation of the [long-form resume](resume-full.md) made before the owner joined GreatCall: same Poway/Skype contact block, no GreatCall or Best Buy Health section at all, seven achievement bullets (no embedded bullet), and *Projects Overview* entries trimmed to one to three paragraphs each. It is the direct ancestor of the [primary resume](resume-achievements.md): the primary's *Most prominent achievements* list, *Side Note*, *Projects Overview* wording, and *Education* section are this document plus the 2018–present material.

## Disposition

**Archive; nothing left to harvest.** Every sentence in it appears either verbatim or in improved form in the primary resume, or at greater depth in the long-form resume. Two phrasings are worth remembering only as style precedent:

- "*Details on any of the projects are available upon request.*" — the primary now says "*Please feel free to ask me for stories from any of the projects below.*", which is warmer; keep the primary's.
- Achievement bullets without the embedded/positioning items show what the pre-2018 frame looked like — useful only if a pure ML/data-science tailored variant is ever needed (see [update-workflow.md](../resume/update-workflow.md) § *Tailored variants*).

Its avatar link (`assets/oleg-zhylin-gravatar.png`) dangles after the move; left as-is.
