# Enabled a Qualcomm AWARE CES showcase with observability-capable Lively devices

## What I did

Enabled Qualcomm AWARE's planned CES showcase to use Lively production hardware rather than generic reference devices. I recognized that the demonstration needed more than devices: it depended on the same development and observability-capable configuration previously established for Qualcomm engineers, so I called out the need to prepare the showcase devices consistently with that environment. This connected the earlier observability evaluation and device-identity work to an externally visible partner demonstration involving location services, device information, health and telemetry reporting, observability metrics and partner-device visibility.

Coordinated device preparation, shipment and stakeholder alignment across Best Buy Health engineering, leadership and device-logistics teams, Qualcomm marketing and product organizations, and the Qualcomm engineering contacts already working with the devices. I stayed involved through approvals and deployment discussions to keep Qualcomm's technical requirements and Best Buy Health expectations aligned.

The partner specifically wanted commercial Lively devices rather than generic reference hardware. The effort therefore reused the development-device and observability-capable configurations established through prior Qualcomm collaboration, allowing Qualcomm to represent an actual health-and-safety wearable platform in its intended demonstration environment.

After the devices shipped, I followed up with Qualcomm leadership to confirm delivery. Qualcomm confirmed receipt and stated that the devices would be used in the CES demonstration with supporting video materials.

## Why it matters

This turned prior engineering work on device configuration, telemetry and observability into a partner-facing demonstration opportunity. It showed that the technical foundation could support a real device representation in Qualcomm's AWARE experience, while extending a relationship previously centered on SDK integration, device identity and observability evaluation into product and technology-marketing coordination.

## Skills demonstrated

External technical partnership; embedded-device deployment; observability-environment configuration; telemetry and location-service integration; cross-functional stakeholder alignment; device logistics; follow-through across engineering and business teams.
