## What I did

Brainstormed an AI-enabled Alation Data Product with Rodd, building on a Data Catalog demonstration already advertised to internal audiences. The original mandate was to determine whether we are wasting warehouse space and cellular data on DA Events that are not needed.

Investigated or considered:

- Intelligent Search and Allie AI, including whether the available Alation edition supports them and whether a metadata upgrade is required.
- Metadata improvements such as tagging, domains, popularity signals from Snowflake query logs, top users, and top table sizes.
- A Data Product that makes data lineage useful by connecting lineage with usage and cost-oriented evidence.
- Chat With Your Data and Alation's Data Product Builder, including the Data Product Builder Copilot and whether a product can be recreated with more complex criteria.
- Whether a system prompt for a Data Product exists, possibly in YAML, and whether an existing Data Product can be edited to add capabilities.
- Reworking existing queries as Data Products instead of notebooks, including a Snowflake notebook about the devices on which R5 data lands and whether it could serve the broader Lively device family.
- Other recurring questions that currently require people to search across multiple data locations.

The proposed Data Product Copilot guidance would explain DA Events, leverage glossaries, and help users interpret glossary terms in column descriptions. The product should demonstrate concrete examples, inspire new use cases, and make the case for the value of warehousing each DA Event table while being explicit about waste, incorrectness, and uncertainty.

## Why it matters

If demonstrated successfully, this would turn an already-advertised Data Catalog effort into a concrete Data Product milestone that could be presented to the same audience and to new audiences. It would connect catalog metadata, lineage, usage, cost, and AI-assisted exploration around a practical question: which DA Events are worth retaining and why?

Potential next audience: Abby and other stakeholders who need Chat With Your Data capabilities and a clearer path from catalog metadata to useful analysis.

## Skills demonstrated

Data-product discovery, data catalog strategy, metadata and glossary design, data lineage, Snowflake usage analysis, AI-assisted analytics, user-oriented prompt design, and honest communication of data quality and cost tradeoffs.
