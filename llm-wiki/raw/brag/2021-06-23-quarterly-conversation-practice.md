# Ran a deliberate quarterly-conversation practice for five years running

## What I did

I have run the company's **Quarterly Conversation** cycle as a deliberate, prepared practice every quarter since at least mid-2021 — through Q4 FY2024, Q2 FY2027, and every quarter in between — rather than treating it as a form to fill in before a deadline.

What that looks like in practice:

- **Carrying commitments across quarters.** The QC format explicitly asks you to refer back to the actions you committed to last time. I maintain the thread deliberately: goals are copied forward quarter to quarter and cleaned up as they change, so each conversation starts from what was actually agreed rather than from memory.
- **Preparing the input rather than improvising it.** I set aside time before each cycle to read back and prime against the goals already recorded, so the accomplishments and key learnings I bring are specific and evidenced. One of my own notes describes submitting "a stellar QC" — the difference is entirely preparation.
- **Keeping a standing topic list for the people I meet regularly**, revisited periodically rather than reconstructed each time. Some threads run for years.
- **Using the ritual to move real questions**, not just to report status — scope, promotion readiness, how the organization is handling advancement, and where my work should point next.
- **Rating my own leadership and performance against the company's published criteria**, deliberately and against the rubric, rather than defaulting to a comfortable middle.

A conversation I recorded as the best I had ever had was one where two leaders attended instead of one — a signal I read as the practice paying off in the attention it attracted.

## Why it matters

Two reasons, and the second is the one that compounds.

**It is how senior scope actually gets negotiated.** Architecture ownership, promotion, and the boundaries of a principal-level role are not granted in a code review. They are worked out in exactly this kind of recurring conversation, and arriving prepared with evidence — carried forward, specific, tied to commitments already made — is what makes those conversations move.

**It is a capture ritual that already exists.** The QC asks, every quarter: *what are the big wins and key learnings you are proud of since your last Quarterly Conversation?* That is the brag file's question, asked by the employer, on a reliable cadence. Answering it well has kept a record of five years of work that would otherwise have been reconstructed from memory — and it is the natural quarterly trigger for capturing entries here (see [the brag workflow](../../wiki/workflows/brag-file.md) § *The quarterly hook*).

## Skills demonstrated

Managing upward deliberately, longitudinal goal tracking, self-assessment against a published rubric, preparation discipline, using organizational rituals as leverage rather than overhead, sustaining professional relationships across years.
