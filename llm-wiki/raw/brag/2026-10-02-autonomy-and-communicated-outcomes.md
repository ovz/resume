# Autonomy taken and given, results made known with the credit handed on — ownership as a working practice

## What I did

In the owner's words (2026-10-02): **"I was always good at autonomy and quickly learned that results not communicated don't exist. So my outcomes are always eagerly advertised and I make sure to maximize impact by giving recipients the autonomy credit. I see great fundamental value in individuals and interactions."**

And, on how it is to be told: **"all outwards materials (stories, resume, other categories including future ones) must reflect achievements in valuable behaviors such as ones we work with in this session. Job Search is genre to cast me in most positive light, strong promise of me being a valuable member well integrated into the organization etc."** That second statement is now rule 3 of [voice and prominence](../../wiki/workflows/voice-and-prominence.md).

This is a **capability entry**, like [2026-09-16 stewardship as a first principle](2026-09-16-stewardship-first-principle.md): it records the practice and indexes the evidence already in the record, behaviour by behaviour, rather than restating it. Stewardship is about the condition of what is handed on; this entry is about *how* it is handed on — the result made known, and the people who receive it left free to own it.

> # Inbox note — factory-mindset lens (2026-09-24)
>
> **From:** Oleg, dictated to Cowork during CoAspire prep. **Type:** theme / positioning input.
> **Not** a resume claim, and **must not** appear on the resume, About Me or dream-job text as an argument. Oleg cannot say it explicitly to employers; he is developing an eye for it.
>
> ## The theme
>
> "AI factory" is becoming a glamour word. Well-run manufacturing is admirable and soothing, but bringing it up and keeping it up is tedious and historically thankless — and the risk is a "glorified factory" where software engineers are conveyor-belt workers, overseers glamorise the belt and gaslight friction, and workers slowly degrade. Oleg's counter-position is autonomy, stewardship and ownership (the same thread as the Sep 23 "why here" story).
>
> ## What it changes on the resume side
>
> - **Select bullets that show judgment, not throughput.** Prefer "decided / owned / retired the risk / stopped the line" over "delivered N features" or ticket-count language.
> - **Wording:** favour ownership verbs and outcomes; avoid passive-executor phrasing ("responsible for", "tasked with", "supported").
> - **About Me / dream job:** the stewardship-and-ownership story stands on its own; do not frame it as opposition to anything. Show the autonomy mindset through stories, never a stated thesis.
> - **Filtering, not writing:** the lens is mainly for evaluating roles and postings (whose language is outcome-based, whose is throughput-based), so it belongs in job-search notes more than in the resume text itself.
>
> ## Provenance and open items
>
> - Podcast remarks (Russ White on US-military autonomy; Jocko Willink on ownership) are Oleg's recollection and unverified. Do not quote them in any deliverable without checking the source.
> - The company-side version lives in the cpp_edu space: `jobs/coaspire/primers/factory-mindset-lens.md`.
> - Suggested repo location: the resume repo's inbox directory (path assumed — adjust to the repo's convention).

### Vocabulary a defence listener already speaks — checked 2026-10-02

The owner named the U.S. military's "people, not cogs" outlook as useful for defence-sector conversations. The podcasts he remembers it from were **not** checked and are not cited. These public sources carry the same idea and were checked:

- **The SOF Truths** ([U.S. Special Operations Command](https://www.socom.mil/socpac/Pages/SOF-Truths.aspx)). Truth 1: "Humans are more important than hardware" — "People – not equipment – make the critical difference." Truth 2: "Quality is better than quantity." Truth 3: "Special Operations Forces cannot be mass produced."
- **Mission command**, Army Doctrine Publication 6-0 (2019): "the Army's approach to command and control that empowers subordinate decision making and decentralizes execution appropriate to the situation" ([army.mil, 2019-08-08](https://www.army.mil/article/225414/combined_arms_center_launches_new_mission_command_doctrine)). Its seven principles — competence, mutual trust, shared understanding, commander's intent, mission orders, disciplined initiative, risk acceptance — were confirmed from secondary sources citing ADP 6-0; the publication itself was not reachable. Disciplined initiative includes reporting the situation while acting on the intent, which is "results not communicated don't exist" in doctrinal form.
- **The Agile Manifesto** (2001), first value: "Individuals and interactions over processes and tools" — the owner's "great fundamental value in individuals and interactions", and the same Manifesto the [hardware-cadence entry](2026-09-11-hardware-cadence-engineer-to-engineer.md) already cites.

How the stories map onto this vocabulary is in the [About me hub](../../wiki/stories/about-me.md) § *Ownership and autonomy*.

## Why it matters

- **It is the "well integrated" half of the job-search promise.** Stewardship shows what he leaves behind; these behaviours show how he works among other people while doing it — which is what a hiring team is actually trying to predict.
- **It is well grounded.** Every behaviour has at least four independent entries behind it, several already on the resume; this entry adds the practice and the owner's statement, not new claims of fact.
- **It is what mission-command organizations screen for.** Initiative inside an intent, reporting while acting, trust extended to the people who receive the work — a defence employer will recognise the behaviours without being told the doctrine.
- **It is uncommon in an individual contributor.** Presenting results across teams, handing an expert role on, and naming what recipients made of a handover are usually treated as a manager's work.

## Skills demonstrated

Ownership and initiative; judgment under organizational pressure; communicating results across teams and upward; delegation and handover; mentoring through questions rather than verdicts; cross-boundary engineer-to-engineer collaboration; giving credit by role.

## Evidence

The linked entries in the table, the primary resume's GreatCall and Salford sections, and the owner's statements of 2026-10-02 and 2026-09-24. External vocabulary: the three public sources linked above.

## Evidence limitations

- **The practice is self-described.** What is evidenced is the behaviour in each row, not the disposition; outward it is *shown* through those episodes, never asserted — the rule [voice and prominence](../../wiki/workflows/voice-and-prominence.md) already applies to stewardship and to humility, respect and trust.
- **"Giving recipients the autonomy credit" is thinner than the rest.** The record shows handovers, invitations to co-own, credit by role in the resume text and thanks to a partner's engineers. It does not show public credit to a named person on a specific occasion. Tell the handover and what the recipients did; do not claim a ceremony.
- **"Results not communicated don't exist" is the owner's own saying** (2026-10-02). Per [spoken drafts](../../wiki/workflows/spoken-drafts.md) rule 3 it may appear once in a story, introduced as his, never as the closing line.
- **No military service is on record.** The defence vocabulary is the listener's, not his: never imply service, and never recite doctrine at a defence interviewer. Mission-command principles are confirmed from secondary sources only.

## What was blocked, cut short, or wrong

- **Handing autonomy on has limits.** Some of the QA engineers he mentored were complying rather than curious, and the mandate's schedule gave the inexperienced no time to follow curiosity ([2024-12-31](2024-12-31-device-test-automation-robot-framework.md)). He could choose what to guide; he could not supply the time.
- **Not every case made was a case won.** The 2017 staffing argument for an outsourced team was made with a product rationale, and the mail does not show who was retained ([2015-01-01](2015-01-01-mirabit-outsourcing-vendor-staffing-management.md)); the coverage-metric pushback was resisted rather than settled on the record.
