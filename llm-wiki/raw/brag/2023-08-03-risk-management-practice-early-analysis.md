## What I did

Personal planning note capturing early analysis of the organization's Risk Management practice. Reviewed standard risk-response terminology (risk disposition: accept, avoid, monitor, mitigate, transfer) against the organization's guidance, and noted that documented risk-management planning existed for only one line of business rather than consistently across the org. Planned to contribute a unified, cross-line approach aligned with existing product-development practice, and to propose clearer names for the organization's risk categories. Also planned to engage a colleague about whether a formal quality-management or issue-tracking system (e.g., a tool like Qualio or Jira) should support the practice going forward, and to build shared understanding of a couple of systems-thinking concepts leadership was using in Quality discussions, and of stakeholders with a history in the space, ahead of contributing further.

## Why it matters

Identified a concrete, org-wide gap: risk assessments relied on qualitative judgment with little quantification of probability, and risk-management planning was uneven across lines of business. Proposed risk appetite as a foundational concept for the organization's overall risk-management approach — moving the practice from ad hoc, single-line planning toward a consistent, numerically-informed, org-wide discipline.
