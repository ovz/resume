# Mastered hardware's rigid cadence alongside iterative software, through engineer-to-engineer relationships

## What I did

In the owner's words: **"One of the highlights of my experience: I mastered the rigid cyclicity of hardware, where pre-planning is where agile iterations are, and the actual software engineering agile iterative approach. My best success was when I established engineer-to-engineer relationships, and the classic Agile Manifesto contains everything one needs to practice solid engineer-to-engineer relationships and scale this into a transferable skill."** And: **"People who know will understand that devices with new hardware, industrial etc. design run on at least a six-month cadence."**

Three things held together:

- **Hardware's rhythm is rigid.** A device with new hardware and industrial design runs on a cycle of six months or more, and it cannot be hurried by process. Its agility lives in the **pre-planning** — that is where iteration pays, before the cycle commits.
- **Software's rhythm is iterative**, running inside the hardware cycle. Mastering both, and the seam between them, is the skill.
- **Engineer-to-engineer relationships** are what make the seam work, across the organizational boundary as much as within a team. The record has them with the manufacturer ([2022-08-03 specifications](2022-08-03-odm-specification-authoring.md), the [manufacturer transition](2023-09-01-r5-odm-transition.md), the [FOTA escalation](2025-07-18-fota-vendor-escalation-lively-mobile2.md)), with silicon partners on positioning ([2024-05-15](2024-05-15-skyhook-positioning-root-cause-diagnostics.md)), and with firmware engineering ([2025-05-29](2025-05-29-r5-anomaly-monitor-validated-against-mcu-alerts.md)).

**The Agile Manifesto as the framework.** Its four values — *individuals and interactions over processes and tools; working software over comprehensive documentation; customer collaboration over contract negotiation; responding to change over following a plan* — are read here as a description of how two engineers on either side of a contract boundary get work done. That reading is what turns a personal knack into a transferable skill. (Manifesto: <https://agilemanifesto.org/>.)

**Where it breaks.** *Customer collaboration over contract negotiation* is the value a hybrid integration model forces into reverse: a device that is too specialized to swap vendors on, yet not owned closely enough to fix in-house, turns every gap in engineer-to-engineer collaboration into a contract negotiation running at hardware pace. The owner's statement of that pitfall, and its grounding in transaction cost economics and switching-cost theory, is in [2025-04-13 breadth-first white label](2025-04-13-ble-sdk-breadth-first-white-label.md). The same instinct shows in [2020-07-14](2020-07-14-upward-feedback-practice.md): kanbanizing a process that was Scrum in name only.

## Why it matters

- **It is the practice a principal hardware-software engineer is hired for**, and it transfers to any organization that ships devices.
- **It explains the results elsewhere in the record**: specifications usable across a language boundary, an escalation resolved by acquiring depth, and a manufacturer transition that returned to a normal lifecycle.
