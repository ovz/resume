# Fixed degrading location on the previous-generation wearable through engineer-to-engineer work with the manufacturer's team, and became the company's positioning expert

## What I did

**The owner, 2026-09-14** (the full note is preserved in [2022-09-16](2022-09-16-skyhook-license-buy-calendar-time.md)): *"Positioning is mostly a success, things done right, I fixed locations by working with Borqs India in proper engineer to engineer collaboration."*

**The owner's 2021 interview-preparation document**, as quoted in the about-me repository's 2026-09-24 voice snapshot, adds the shape: cellular networks and satellite positioning were new domains to him when the location quality of the devices started to deteriorate; he built himself a curriculum, reached the root cause after about half a year of experiments, had a suspicion early that the chip vendor later confirmed — *"I was also correct in my suspicions… This was confirmed by Qualcomm"* — and was introduced to the chip vendor as the company's positioning subject-matter expert.

## Why it matters

It is the origin of the positioning thread that runs through the rest of the record, and an early instance of the working style he names as his best: engineer-to-engineer collaboration across a manufacturer boundary, rather than escalation through contracts.

## Skills demonstrated

Learning a new technical domain fast and deliberately; root-cause analysis across device, modem and vendor; engineer-to-engineer collaboration with a manufacturer's team.

## Evidence

The owner's statement of 2026-09-14; his 2021 interview-preparation document (personal Google Drive), quoted in the about-me voice snapshot. The 2024 R4 location-data work on the device-programme board shows the same device's positioning still under his eye years later.
