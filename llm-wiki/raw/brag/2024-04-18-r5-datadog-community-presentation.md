# Presented Datadog observability program to engineering community, caught a live issue on stage

- date: 2024-04-18
- context: Best Buy Health, cross-team engineering community-of-practice presentation on the device observability program
- domains: operational excellence and observability, leadership and communication
- sensitivity: private-repo
- resume-worthy: yes

## What I did

Presented the device's Datadog observability program to a cross-team engineering community of practice, framing the "Dashboards vs. Automated Alerts" tradeoff (dashboards do not scale past the humans watching them; tuned alerts do) and walking through the two fundamental production monitors plus a growing set of ad hoc, condition-specific monitors added as the fleet grew. During the live session, used a monitor to investigate an unusual spike in personal alert volume in real time and identified, on the spot, a single misbehaving production device generating an abnormal volume of location-service errors — a live catch performed in front of the audience. Closed by inviting other teams to subscribe to and help tune the monitors going forward.

## Why it matters

Elevated a team-level operational-excellence effort into cross-team visibility and adoption, reframed it as reusable "operational excellence" guidance rather than a one-off deliverable, and demonstrated — live, unscripted — that the monitoring investment paid off with a real proactive catch, on top of an earlier proactive catch of a battery-overheating condition shortly after launch.

## Skills demonstrated

Technical presentation to a mixed engineering audience, live troubleshooting/triage under observation, monitoring-program stewardship, cross-team influence.
